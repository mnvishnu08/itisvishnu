Years ago, we did not have bank apps this good.

The new Kotak Bank app is pure bliss.

When I open the app, I can switch on a setting that enables QR as the first screen. This is perfect, as most of us use these apps frequently for QR payments every day.

Now I can scan here, and on the next page, I can enter the amount and click pay. Face ID automatically authenticates, and my payment is done.

In case it fails, there is a failsafe mechanism to do it through the PIN as well.

This is the most seamless experience for me — the fastest and easiest, directly on my bank app.

Glad to see banks now innovating and trying to compete with the major players 🙂
