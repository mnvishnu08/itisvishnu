I've been going to the same coffee shop for months, and I just realized it's a masterclass in UX design.

## The Setup

Small place. Two baristas. Always packed. But somehow, the line moves fast and everyone leaves happy.

## The Magic

**1. Clear Visual Hierarchy**
- Menu is simple: 6 drinks, 3 sizes
- Prices are big and visible
- No decision paralysis

**2. Optimized Flow**
- Order → Pay → Wait area → Pickup
- Each step is obvious
- No confusion about where to go

**3. Feedback Loops**
- Barista calls your name when ready
- You can see them making your drink
- Clear status updates

**4. Error Prevention**
- They repeat your order back
- Write your name on the cup
- Ask about allergies upfront

## The Lesson

Great UX isn't about fancy animations or complex features. It's about removing friction and making the user's journey as smooth as possible.

Sometimes the best product insights come from the most unexpected places.
