# MBA Capstone: Fintech Strategy

During my MBA at IIM Ahmedabad, I worked with a fintech startup to develop a comprehensive go-to-market strategy for SME lending in emerging markets.

## Project Scope

- **Market Analysis**: Deep dive into SME lending landscape in 3 emerging markets
- **Competitive Intelligence**: Analysis of 15+ competitors and their strategies
- **Business Model Design**: Revenue streams, cost structure, and unit economics
- **Go-to-Market Strategy**: Customer acquisition, partnerships, and scaling plan
- **Financial Projections**: 5-year financial model with scenario planning

## Key Deliverables

- **Market Research Report**: 80-page comprehensive market analysis
- **Business Strategy Presentation**: Executive summary for board presentation
- **Financial Model**: Dynamic Excel model with sensitivity analysis
- **Implementation Roadmap**: 18-month execution plan with milestones

## Research Methodology

- **Primary Research**: 50+ interviews with SME owners and lenders
- **Secondary Research**: Industry reports, regulatory analysis, competitor study
- **Data Analysis**: Python for data processing and visualization
- **Financial Modeling**: Advanced Excel with Monte Carlo simulations

## Key Insights

- **Market Gap**: 40% of SMEs underserved by traditional lenders
- **Technology Opportunity**: Digital-first approach could reduce costs by 60%
- **Partnership Strategy**: Collaboration with existing players more effective than competition
- **Regulatory Considerations**: Compliance framework critical for scaling

## Recommendations

1. **Phased Market Entry**: Start with one market, prove model, then expand
2. **Partnership-First Approach**: Work with existing financial institutions
3. **Technology Investment**: Build proprietary risk assessment algorithms
4. **Customer Education**: Significant investment needed in financial literacy

## Impact

- Startup raised $2M Series A funding based on strategy
- Implemented 80% of recommendations in first year
- Achieved break-even 6 months ahead of projections
- Expanded to second market within 18 months

## Skills Developed

- **Strategic Thinking**: Long-term planning and scenario analysis
- **Market Research**: Primary and secondary research methodologies
- **Financial Modeling**: Advanced Excel and financial analysis
- **Stakeholder Management**: Working with diverse teams and interests
- **Presentation Skills**: Communicating complex ideas to executives

## Lessons Learned

- **Customer-Centricity**: Deep customer understanding is foundation of good strategy
- **Regulatory Complexity**: Fintech regulations vary significantly across markets
- **Execution Matters**: Great strategy means nothing without proper execution
- **Iteration Required**: Strategy must evolve based on market feedback
